Request an App应用程式申请

Only apps on the school's approved MDM whitelist may be installed on student devices. To request a new educational app, follow the steps below.
只有列入学校 MDM 批准白名单的应用程式才可安装于学生设备。如需申请新的教育应用程式，请按以下步骤进行。

How it works · 申请流程

  1. Find the app in the App Store and confirm its exact name. 在应用商店中确认所需应用程式的名称。
  2. Check the approved list first — it may already be available. 先查看“白名单”，确认该应用是否已获批准。
  3. Submit the request form with the app name and its educational purpose. 填写申请表，说明应用名称及其教育用途。
  4. Review — the MDM team responds within 3 school working days; approved apps are added to the whitelist and you install from the App Store yourself. MDM 团队将于 3 个学校工作日内审核；获批准的应用会加入白名单，学生需自行从应用商店安装。
Submit a Request 提交申请 Check the Approved List 查看白名单

Not approved but you think it's important? Reply to the review with more detail, or contact the IT Department. 若未获批准但你认为该应用重要，请提供进一步理由，或联系资讯科技部。
